Journal Week 8:


Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?
The client for the DriverPass project was DriverPass, a company that helps students prepare for driving tests. They wanted me to design a system where customers could take online courses and practice driving tests, schedule driving lessons, and view their progress.

What did you do particularly well?
I did well understanding the system’s requirements and turning them into UML diagrams. 


If you could choose one part of your work to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?
I would revise my UML diagrams to make them clearer and easier to follow by organizing the labels better and color coding certain labels to make them easier to read. This would help give developers clearer instructions for building the system.


How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it important to consider the user’s needs?
I reviewed the client’s requirements to understand what customers and employees needed from DriverPass. I included features for scheduling driving lessons, taking practice tests, and tracking progress in my system design to address user needs. It is important to consider user needs because it helps ensure the system meets the clients’ expectations and addresses all of their concerns


How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future?
I start by understanding the requirements and breaking the desired functionality down into smaller objects and functions. In future projects, I would use UML diagrams, review designs with users, and test early to find issues that need to be addressed.
